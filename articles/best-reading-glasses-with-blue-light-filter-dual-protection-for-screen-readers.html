Best Reading Glasses with Blue Light Filter — Dual Protection for Screen Readers

August 05, 2026 · 6 min read

Most people over 40 need reading glasses. Most people over 40 also spend hours on screens. Yet the standard drugstore reader does nothing about blue light — it just magnifies text. Here are the best reading glasses that combine magnification with blue light filtering, so you are not swapping between two different pairs all day.

Why Combine Readers and Blue Light Filter?

If you read on a Kindle, iPad, or phone — as most people do — you are getting a double dose of eye strain: presbyopic focusing effort AND blue light exposure. Separate reading glasses and blue light glasses means constant swapping. Combined glasses solve both problems in one frame.

Our Top Picks

1. Gaoye Blue Light Blocking Reading Glasses — Best Overall

Gaoye has taken their popular reading glasses line and added an effective blue light filtering coating. Available in all standard strengths (+1.00 to +3.50), the lenses filter approximately 40% of blue light while maintaining good color accuracy. The 5-pack option gives you different styles for different settings — keep a pair at your desk, bedside, and living room. At roughly $4-5 per pair in the multi-pack, the value is unbeatable.

2. Gamma Ray Blue Light Readers — Best Lens Quality

Gamma Ray's blue light readers use a higher-grade polycarbonate lens with anti-reflective coating AND blue light filtering in a single lens. The result is noticeably less glare than the Gaoye lenses, especially under office fluorescent lighting. Available in classic rectangular and round frame styles. Slightly more expensive (~$15-18 per pair) but worth it for the improved optics.

3. ThinOptics Blue Light Reading Glasses — Best Portable

ThinOptics has added a blue light filtering version to their ultra-thin readers that stick to your phone case. Same incredible portability — 0.15 inches thin, always with you — now with screen protection. The nose-bridge design takes getting used to, but for quick reading tasks on your phone, nothing is more convenient.

Reading Glasses Strength + Screen Distance

Remember: if you use these primarily for computer screens (20-26 inches) rather than books/phones (14-16 inches), order 0.50 to 0.75 weaker than your standard reading strength. Using full reading strength at computer distance causes unnecessary eye strain.
