Best Prescription Glasses for High Myopia — Thin Lenses for Strong Prescriptions

August 07, 2026 · 7 min read

If your prescription is above -4.00 (or +3.00 for hyperopia), you know the struggle: 'standard' lenses come out thick as glass bottle bottoms. The edges protrude from frames, the weight hurts your nose, and the 'Coke bottle' magnification effect makes your eyes look tiny or huge. Here is how to get thin, comfortable glasses with a strong prescription — and where to order them online.

Zenni's frame selection includes smaller round and oval frames ideal for minimizing lens thickness — crucial because lens thickness is most visible at the edges, and smaller frames have less edge area.

EyeBuyDirect's high-index pricing is competitive, and their frequent BOGO/30% off sales bring 1.67 lenses under $30 per pair. Their 'Premium Progressive' 1.67 lenses for those who need both high-index and multifocal correction are a standout option that few online competitors match at this price.

3. LensFactory — Lens Replacement Service

If you have frames you already love, LensFactory will install new high-index prescription lenses — including 1.74 — into your existing frames. Starting at $97 for 1.67 single-vision, this is pricier than Zenni but cheaper than a new pair from a traditional optical shop.

Frame Choice Matters as Much as Lens Index

Even with 1.74 lenses, a large square frame will expose thick lens edges. For strong prescriptions:

  • Choose smaller, rounder frames: Less edge area = less visible thickness
  • Thicker plastic frames hide edges better than thin metal frames
  • Avoid semi-rimless or rimless: No frame to hide the lens edge
  • Choose frames where your eyes are centered in the lens opening: Off-center eyes mean more edge thickness on one side
